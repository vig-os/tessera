# [PR 938](https://github.com/vig-os/tessera/pull/938) feat(core): stored-bytes seal groundwork (ADR-0066 A′, part 1 of 2)

Part 1 of 2 of ADR-0066 implementation step 4. **Additive**: no format byte changes, no writer or reader
switches, no corpus, golden or seal-case change. The part-2 cutover follows and switches every writer and reader
to these members at once (the layout changes exactly once, there).

## What this adds
- `tessera-core::stored`: member names (`manifest.json`, `manifest.seal`, `collection.json`, `collection.seal`),
  `SEAL_LEN`, `seal_name_for`, and `parse_seal` (exactly `blake3:<64 lower-case hex>`, 71 bytes).
- `Error::UnsupportedLayout`: a document found without its seal member is the pre-ADR-0066 alpha layout, refused
  (rule 10, no migration) naming the declared `tessera_version`, the missing seal member, an in-band
  `manifest_hash`, a non-JCS layout, and the layout required. `Error::MalformedSeal`: a seal member in another format.
- `Manifest::from_stored_members` / `Collection::from_stored_members`: read from the two members (seal optional),
  then `from_stored`.
- `tessera-io::stored::{seal_path, write_pair, read_pair}`: the pair on disk (`.seal` beside `.json`). Unused by the
  crate until part 2; public, documented and tested.
- Tests: #933 item 1 (`from_stored` refuses forged sealed bytes with a wrong `content_hash`, no `content_hash`, or a
  wrong `id`, naming the field, for manifest and collection); `from_stored_members` over every tracked product
  (refused without a seal member, naming both formats; read with one; malformed seal members refused); the on-disk
  pair round trip and its refusals.

## Proof
- Additive: `git diff origin/dev...HEAD -- tessera/corpus '**/*.tsra' '**/*.trycmd' corpus` is empty; the diff is
  526 insertions, 1 deletion (an import line).
- `cargo nextest run -p tessera-core -p tessera-io`: 583/583 passed. Commit hooks: workspace `clippy -D warnings` and
  rustfmt passed. The full workspace suite runs in CI.
- Pre-change failure: with #933's mutant applied (`verify_received_id_and_content()` deleted from
  `Manifest::from_stored`), `from_stored_refuses_forged_identity_and_content_hashes` fails
  (`stored_bytes.rs:638`, `1 test run: 0 passed, 1 failed`).

Toward #914 (part 2 is the cutover); addresses #933 item 1.
