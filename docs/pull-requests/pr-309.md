# [PR 309](https://github.com/vig-os/tessera/pull/309) feat(cli): recursive collection verify — descend into sub-collection members (ADR-0049 Part 2)

## Why

#306 gave collections a `kind: Collection` member (recursion mechanism); #282 gave the flat consumer verbs. Both merged to spike. This is **Part 2** — the verbs now *honor* nested members, completing the recursion feature end-to-end (`project → dataset → products`).

## What

- **`member_path` resolves by `kind`** — `<ref>.tsra` (product) vs `<ref>.collection.json` (sub-collection), per ADR-0049 §4.
- **`verify` is recursive (ADR-0049 §3)** — the collection seal, then per member: a product opens its `.tsra` (own seal + pinned version); a **sub-collection descends into its `collection.json`** and verifies it transitively (its seal + the pinned version + its whole subtree). **Cycle guard** (defense-in-depth — content-addressing is acyclic by construction). **Offline-honest** — an unreachable child fails loudly, never a silent pass. Reports the recursive member count.
- **`inspect`** shows each member's kind (`product`/`collection`); **`ls`** resolves a sub-collection member's human name from its child `collection.json`.

## Evidence

A `project → dataset → products` nest: `verify` recurses (3 members), `inspect` shows the `collection` kind, `ls` shows the child's name, and a tampered **grandchild** `.tsra` is caught **transitively**. Full `tessera-cli` suite green; `nix flake check`: **all checks passed!**

Closes the recursion work under #293 (mechanism #306 + verbs #282 + this).
