# [PR 767](https://github.com/vig-os/tessera/pull/767) test(io): add the ksb RSS proof jobs for push_table (ADR-0064 §8.1)

Refs: #715

## What

The ksb jobs for ADR-0064 §8.1's RSS proof of `StreamWriter::push_table` (PR G, part 1 of 2), against the criteria
as amended by #760 (PR #764). `tessera/bench/ksb/677-rss-proof/`:

- `flake.nix` builds the consumer reproducer (MorePET/visionx-rs PR #41 at `c4aff94`, `stream_memory.rs`, pinned by
  full SHA) with its own nixpkgs and `Cargo.lock`, tessera-core and tessera-io patched to dev at `26159de` (the #759
  fix, PR #765). The private visionx input is fetched submit-side and shipped as a store path; no credential enters
  a job. Apps: `default` (criteria 1–5) and `parallel` (criterion 6).
- `stream_memory.patch` switches the reproducer's `pairs` job to `push_table` (exact nullable schema, `ms`
  nullable), accepts `--rows 0`, and adds `--session-dir` and a 10 ms CSV sampler (`RssAnon`, the cgroup's
  `memory.current` and `memory.stat` `anon`/`file`/`shmem`).
- `run.py` runs and evaluates the two jobs. Each exits 1 on any failed criterion. A criterion with missing or
  failed runs fails, so nothing passes on no data.
  - `job`: criteria 1–5, staged on disk-backed `--scratch`. Session and output dirs must not be tmpfs.
    - The W = 4 points at 64 blocks or more run k = 3 times.
    - Criterion 1 is checked on every run with `c_leaf · n`, where `c_leaf = 2 · (e_leaf + 1 KiB)` and `e_leaf` is
      the largest `pairs` leaf entry in that run's sealed manifest, as RFC 8785 JSON.
    - Criterion 2 compares medians of 3 at W = 4.
    - Criterion 5 compares the canonical manifests with exactly the §6 fields removed, plus `content_hash`.
    - The disk wall times and `%util` at 268 M rows are printed as `CONTEXT` and never checked (A.4).
  - `parallel`: criterion 6, with the converse guard (session and output dirs must be tmpfs, `/dev/shm`) and 4 CPUs
    per run.
- `flake.nix` (root) declares `run.py` in `scriptsNotRunInCi` with its reason. Without that, `test-coverage` (#520)
  fails it as an orphan (shown below).

## Decisions

- **The r2 diagnosis tooling (`diag.py`, `diag.patch`, the flake's `diag` build) is dropped.** The evaluator doesn't
  use it: it attributed the r1 failures (heaptrack, per-thread CPU) and has done its job. The diagnosis note moves
  to the records PR (part 2), which keeps the commit the tooling lived at.
- **`e_leaf` follows the ADR's definition, not its worked figure.** The canonical entry of a consumer leaf is
  2,964 B, not the 3,180 B in A.1, so `c_leaf` = 7,976 B, not 8,408 B. That is stricter, not looser.
  `run.py`'s JCS reproduces a sealed product's `manifest_hash` byte for byte, so the length is tessera's own. Part 2
  corrects the ADR's figure.
- **Size.** About 780 lines:
  - `stream_memory.patch` (142) is the consumer diff the ADR names;
  - `flake.lock` (80) is generated;
  - `run.py` (~460, ruff-formatted) holds the six criteria, two guards and two jobs.
  Splitting further would separate an evaluator from the job that feeds it.

## Proof

`tessera-715-r3-par-3` (criterion 6) and the fail-before job `tessera-715-r3-failbefore-2` (pre-#759 writer,
