# [PR 767](https://github.com/vig-os/tessera/pull/767) test(io): add the ksb RSS proof jobs for push_table (ADR-0064 §8.1)

Refs: #715

## What

ADR-0064 PR G, part 1 of 2: the ksb jobs and the evaluator for §8.1's RSS proof of `StreamWriter::push_table`, with
criterion 2 as amended by #797 (PR #829; merge that first). `tessera/bench/ksb/677-rss-proof/`:

- `flake.nix` builds the consumer reproducer (MorePET/visionx-rs `c4aff94`, `stream_memory.rs`, pinned by full SHA)
  against tessera `26159de` (the #759 fix, PR #765). The private input is fetched submit-side; no credential enters a
  job. Apps: `default` (criteria 1–5) and `parallel` (criterion 6). The header comment is the run recipe.
- `stream_memory.patch` switches the reproducer's `pairs` job to `push_table` and adds `--session-dir` and a 10 ms
  sampler (`RssAnon`, the cgroup's `memory.current` and `memory.stat`).
- `run.py` runs and evaluates the jobs; a criterion with missing or failed runs fails.
  - **Criterion 2 is C.2's within-run slope.** Per W, over the tenths of every run with `x_rt ≥ 16 W` (the leaves
    whose input is complete at the tenth's end), the fixed-effects fit `y_rt = α_r + b · x_rt`, SE cluster-robust by
    run (CR1), `t₀.₉₅,G−1`. B.3's pass rule is unchanged: slope `b̂ ≤ c_leaf + t · SE`, resolution `2 t SE ≤ δ_z`
    (121,635 B/leaf), and at W = 1 `2 t SE ≤ c_leaf`. Inconclusive fails. B.2's across-run slope is printed as
    `CONTEXT`, never graded.
  - **Shards.** W = 1 runs k₁ = 6 per size (B.5), W = 4 k = 3. `--reps` splits the job; a shard checks only its own
    runs, and `run.py evaluate DIR...` merges them, refusing mixed binaries or duplicate repeats.
  - Criterion 3 keeps the cgroup-baseline fix; criterion 5 compares every run at 268 M rows with W = 1's first.
- `test_run.py` (52 tests, flake check `rss-proof-evaluator`) pins the arithmetic to B.4/B.5/C.4 and the within-run
  fit: flat runs at levels rising with `n` pass within and fail across; a common growth of 2 `c_leaf` fails at both W;
  CR1 against a hand-worked case; G/N/df as C.2 counts them; floors above `δ_z`, or above `c_leaf` at W = 1, fail as
  inconclusive; missing tenths fail.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| r1 `tessera-715-sweep-1` (from its logs) | 1 | 3 | 28 | 19,761 | 15,028 | 14,096 > c_leaf: also inconclusive |
| r1 `tessera-715-sweep-1` (from its logs) | 4 | 2 | 17 | 73,901 | 28,763 | 41,566, resolved |
| `tessera-715-r3-failbefore-2` | 1 | 2 | 18 | 17,219 | 9,237 | 2,513, resolved |

r1 recorded no tenths and its 10 ms CSVs are over `ksb out`'s 1 MiB, but its job logs fetch and hold the reproducer's
~1 s `sample rows= RssAnon=` lines. `run.py log-tenths` rebuilds the tenths from them (same cut as `peaks`);
`fixtures/r1-logs` holds one extract line per tenth for each fitted run, under the source log's sha256, and
`test_run.py` pins the fit. The 52 tests against the pre-change `run.py` (7c873f6): 4 fail, exit 1 (and the 47 of
round 1 against fa09a81: 18 fail).
